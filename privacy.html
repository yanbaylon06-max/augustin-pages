Augustin Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

About Augustin

Augustin is a privately operated personal AI assistant supporting productivity, scheduling and organisation.

Information Accessed

With the account holder's authorisation, Augustin accesses Google Calendar event information, including event titles, dates and times, to provide scheduling assistance.

How Information Is Used

Calendar information is used to respond to scheduling requests and provide relevant assistance. Augustin may process user messages through an AI service to generate responses.

Data Storage and Security

Augustin uses authentication credentials to access authorised services. Access can be revoked through the account holder's Google Account settings.

Data Sharing

Information may be processed by service providers necessary to operate Augustin, including AI, messaging and hosting providers.
